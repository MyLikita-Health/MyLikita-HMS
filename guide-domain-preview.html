Host the booking page on your own domain

Your dedicated booking page normally lives at relay.mylikita.com/book/<your-clinic>. With a custom domain it can live at book.yourclinic.com instead — patients see your brand in the address bar, and the page, QR code and poster all keep working exactly as before.

This guide assumes your booking page is already set up and turned on in Settings → Website Booking Relay. If not, do that first (one click — no console needed), then come back here.

How to set it up

  1. Pick a subdomain. Any subdomain of a domain you own — book.yourclinic.com is the usual choice. Keep it short: it is what patients see and type.
  2. Create a CNAME record in your DNS. Log in to wherever your domain's DNS is managed (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, your web host…) and add a record like this: 
    # DNS record to add
Type:   CNAME
Name:   book            → becomes book.yourclinic.com
Target: relay.mylikita.com
TTL:    default (or 3600)
    The exact labels vary by provider ("Host", "Name", "Alias"…). Do not use a redirect/forwarding feature — it must be a DNS CNAME pointing at the relay.
  3. Wait for DNS to propagate. Usually minutes, occasionally a few hours. You can verify with a public checker (e.g. dnschecker.org) or by opening https://book.yourclinic.com in a private window.
  4. Enter the domain in MyLikita. Open Settings → Website Booking Relay, scroll to Use your own domain, type book.yourclinic.com, and press Save domain.
  5. Share the new link. Once saved, the card shows your custom URL with a copy button. Share it with patients, and use /poster to print an A4 poster with a QR of the custom link.

Good to know

HTTPS is automatic. Once your subdomain resolves to the relay, a certificate is issued for it — no action needed on your side. Until then, keep sharing the relay link; both addresses show the same page.
The relay link keeps working. Your custom domain is an additional address, not a replacement. Old posters and messages pointing at relay.mylikita.com/book/… keep working forever.
Bookings are never affected. Changing or removing the domain only changes the address patients open — appointments already booked stay in the clinic's booking list either way.

One domain per facility, and domains must use HTTPS (the booking pipeline moves patient data). Want the booking form on a page of your existing website instead? See the widget embed guide.
