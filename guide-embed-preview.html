How to embed the booking widget

  1. Copy the ready-made snippet. In MyLikita, open Settings → Website Booking Relay and scroll to Embed on your website. Press Copy snippet. The snippet already contains everything the widget needs for this facility — there is nothing to fill in by hand.
  2. Paste it into your page. Paste just before the closing </body> tag of any page.
    Plain HTML: paste directly into the file. WordPress: a custom HTML block, or a "header & footer" plugin. Wix / Squarespace: an embed / HTML element on the page.
  3. Publish and check. Open the page and confirm the booking form appears and loads the clinic's doctors. The card shows a live preview of the same form before you even touch your website.
  4. Bookings arrive in MyLikita. Patients' requests appear in the clinic's normal booking list. Confirm them and the patient is notified — exactly like every other booking.

What's inside the snippet

Three short options tell the widget where the clinic lives. All three are public by design — the same values already sit in every page that loads the widget. The snippet never contains your secret sync key.

OptionWhat it is
relayUrlThe relay that routes bookings to this clinic (https://relay.mylikita.com or your own relay).
websiteKeyThe clinic's public website key — a client id, not a password. Anyone can see it; only the relay can use it to book into this clinic.
facilityIdThis clinic's public id on the relay.

Rotated your keys? Open the card again and re-copy — the snippet updates with the fresh website key instantly. The old snippet stops working the moment keys rotate.

If your website can never reach this server over the network (for example an offline clinic install), use the CDN bundle instead: the settings card's snippet also works with https://unpkg.com/@mylikita/booking-widget in place of the server-hosted script.

Good to know

No secrets in the page. The snippet is safe to paste anywhere — the website key is a public client id that only lets visitors request appointments for this clinic, never change settings.
Patients don't need accounts. Booking is anonymous until the clinic confirms — patients give a name and phone number, and get notified by SMS/email once confirmed.
Staff confirm everything. No appointment is ever added to the clinic's list without a staff member confirming it first.
