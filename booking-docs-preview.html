Online booking — setup guides

Put MyLikita's secure online booking in front of patients: on your existing website, on a dedicated booking page, or on your own domain. Pick the option that fits this clinic — every one feeds the same appointment list.

Choose how patients book

Both guides below are step-by-step and need no developer. The settings card in MyLikita (Settings → Website Booking Relay) is where each one starts.

Widget embed

Add booking to your website

Put the booking form inline on any page of the clinic's existing website — WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or plain HTML. Visitors book right on your site.

Open the embed guide →
 Custom domain

Host booking on your own domain

Give the dedicated booking page a branded address like book.yourclinic.com instead of relay.mylikita.com/book/… — no website build needed.

The three options at a glance

All three use the same secure pipeline — bookings land in the clinic's normal appointment list for staff to confirm. The difference is only where the patient sees the form.

Not sure where to start? If the clinic has no website, skip the guides entirely — open Settings → Website Booking Relay, press the one-click setup button, and share the booking link, QR code or printed poster. The guides are only for putting that same form somewhere custom.
